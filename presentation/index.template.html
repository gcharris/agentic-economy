The Agentic Economy · the invisible engine

State moves only at a boundary.
Nothing is shared by default.

This is the context engine underneath a game that scales from one room with an oak table to a planetary cryptographic computer. It is not an entity-component system. It is an asynchronous agentic state machine: every tick is a block, every agent drafts in private, and the world changes only when an envelope crosses a gate.

LAW 1

Parallel encapsulation

Agents draft against an owned snapshot. The type system leaves them no reference to reach for.

LAW 2

The Oak Table

State is local to the house. The papers stay on the table; the helper can be replaced.

LAW 3

The Gate

A transition happens only when a proposal crosses a verification boundary. There is no other path.

LAW 4

Sovereignty

Irreversible commits need authorisation: a hand at the door, a swap, a netting run, a ruling, a proof.

Stage 1 · a week with a helper

Three objects, before any economy

The philosophy is small on purpose. Before markets, before reputation, before a new kind of money, there is a purse, a note, and a door. The engine is those three objects made exact.

The purse

“It is not a theory of money. It is the amount you are willing to let them burn, this week, on thinking.”

Compute reserves are local truth. Every lookup, draft and audit is priced in the laboratory's units: one credit is ten tokens is a twentieth of a joule. When the purse is empty, the run stops. It does not throw the work away.

The note

“The note says what they were in the middle of, what they spent, and that they stopped because the purse was empty.”

Exhaustion is a pause, not a death. The engine writes a structured note onto the oak table, keeps every paper, and waits. A top-up continues the week. Nothing is ever deleted for going broke.

The door

“The helper comes to the door and stands there. The purse does not drain while they stand there.”

Looking and drafting never ask. Sending, paying a stranger, signing, deleting the only copy: those wait. In the engine, waiting at the door burns exactly zero credits. Truth keeps decaying while you decide; that is doc 02's rule, not something the battery measured.
